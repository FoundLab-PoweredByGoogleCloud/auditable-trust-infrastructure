Auditable Trust Infrastructure (ATI)

The category for proving what a regulated AI system did, without keeping the data that proves it.

Auditable Trust Infrastructure (ATI) is an architectural category — a Layer-0 standard for how AI, data, and trust interact inside regulated institutions.

It exists to resolve a conflict that policy and process cannot: the law requires you to retain the evidence of a decision for years, and simultaneously requires you to erase the personal data behind it on request.
